Telosian is based on the idea that we can live in harmony with ourselves, each other, and the natural world. 

We don't really have a choice any more. 

Technology is growing at such a pace that it will soon be possible for individuals to destroy civilization, through ai-leveraged attacks on infrastructure, bioweapons, robots, nuclear weapons, or the like. 

We can't stop technology - it will continue to improve, driven by the anticipation of others getting there first. 

So, we need to learn to live with each other and ourselves.

We need

1. Better understanding of our brains, and ways to achieve better harmony - both within and without. We need more comprehensive models of our psyches and social structures, and ways to reduce conflict and suffering. 

1. A social network that extends throughout the world, across language barriers, consisting of real people that you know, and the ones that they know, and on outwards. Everyone you encounter would therefore be a real person, and you will know how you are connected. This will reduce the possibility of AI bots taking over our social networks. This could be built on or adjacent to networks like Bluesky, which is transparent and open. 

1. Reclaiming our attention from social media. Bluesky and the like will help with this, by letting us control the algorithm. Instead of addictive content, we can see recent updates from people we know or care about, or things we've previously liked, etc. 

1. Reducing our need to work to survive. This can include lower cost housing, more self-production of energy, water, and food. Going offgrid while ongrid. Technology can help with all of these. 

1. Better access to mental health care, through AI or otherwise. 

1. Better access to free or affordable education.
